Quiet Bible - study tools (Hebrew & Greek words, cross-references, commentary, dictionary)
=========================================================================================

The study data in assets/www/bible/study/ was built from the sources below by
study/tools/build_study.py in Quiet Bible's source code. The builder reshapes the data (it
groups words by verse, counts how the KJV renders each Hebrew and Greek word, lists the
verses each word appears in, and splits everything into small files). It does not change the
wording of any definition, note or entry, except that markup and some reference lines are
removed, and a few notes are attached to the verse they explain.

Hebrew and Greek words behind the King James Version
  xmlBible.org by Danny Carlton - GNU General Public License, version 3
  https://github.com/DannyCarlton/xmlBible.org (commit 71c681e, September 2026)
  Built by its author from:
    - CrossWire's "KJV (1769) with Strongs Numbers and Morphology" (OSIS edition),
      https://crosswire.org
    - STEPBible TAHOT (Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT) and TAGNT (Translators
      Amalgamated Greek NT), STEPBible.org, Tyndale House, Cambridge - Creative Commons
      Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    - The Cambridge Paragraph Bible (Scrivener, 1873), public domain

Strong's Hebrew and Greek dictionaries
  James Strong, "A Concise Dictionary of the Words in the Hebrew Bible" and "A Concise
  Dictionary of the Words in the Greek Testament" (1890) - public domain.
  XML editions by David Troidl and David Instone-Brewer (Hebrew) and Ulrik Sandborg-Petersen
  (Greek), marked public domain, from Open Scriptures:
  https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs (commit 0acd2f2)

Meanings ("Meaning" in a word study)
  TBESH (Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Hebrew) and TBESG (... for Greek),
  STEPBible.org, Tyndale House, Cambridge - CC BY 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data (commit 0f60797, September 2025).
  Changes: markup removed and verse links turned into plain text.

Cross-references
  OpenBible.info cross references (2025-10-13 edition), based on the Treasury of Scripture
  Knowledge (public domain) and ranked by readers' votes - Creative Commons Attribution
  (CC BY), https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/
  Changes: references with no positive votes are left out.

Commentaries
  Matthew Henry, "An Exposition of the Old and New Testament" (1706-1714) - public domain.
  Robert Jamieson, A. R. Fausset and David Brown, "A Commentary, Critical, Experimental and
  Practical, on the Old and New Testaments" (1871) - public domain.
  Text from the HelloAO Free Use Bible API (marked public domain), as collected by Open
  Christian Data, https://github.com/eve-coda/open-christian-data (data files marked CC0 1.0,
  commit 3733dcc). Matthew Henry's commentary has no notes on the Song of Solomon in this
  edition.

Bible dictionaries
  M. G. Easton, "Illustrated Bible Dictionary" (1897); William Smith, "A Dictionary of the
  Bible" (1863); Roswell D. Hitchcock, "An Interpreting Dictionary of Scripture Proper Names"
  (1869) - all public domain. Entries as parsed from the Christian Classics Ethereal Library's
  editions by the NEUU bible-dictionary-dataset - CC BY 4.0,
  https://github.com/neuu-org/bible-dictionary-dataset (commit b8e82aa).
