Quiet Bible - Berean Standard Bible (built in)
=============================================

The Holy Bible, Berean Standard Bible, BSB is produced in cooperation with Bible Hub, Discovery
Bible, unfoldingWord, Bible Aquifer, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee.
This text of God's Word has been dedicated to the public domain. Free resources and databases
are available at BereanBible.com.

What Quiet Bible did with it (translations/tools/build_bsb.py in the source code):
- The words are those of bsb.txt (https://bereanbible.com/bsb.txt, SHA-256
  2ac3af1de52d4e68261cba91d85c320b7eadc6560e830d99e591767b8ff5ca96), with runs of spaces made
  single. No word is changed.
- Section headings, parallel passages, translator notes, psalm titles, poetry indents and the
  words of Jesus come from the BSB edition Quiet Bible was first built with
  (translations/bsb/structure.json); 18 psalm titles that edition did not mark are marked from
  the text of bsb.txt.
- The 16 verses the BSB leaves out (Matthew 17:21 and others) are left out; the notes on the
  verses before them say why.
- The map (bible-cross-references-notices.txt) shows the same text.

The King James Version, Quiet Bible's main text, is public domain in the United States and most
of the world; in the United Kingdom the right to print it is reserved to the Crown's patentees.
