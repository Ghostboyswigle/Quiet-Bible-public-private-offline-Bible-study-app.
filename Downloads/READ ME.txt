QUIET BIBLE v1.4.2
==================

A private, offline Bible reader for Android that reads aloud. No account, no ads, no tracking,
and no internet permission. Needs Android 8 or later. The app is about 74 MB.

1. Install the app
   Open QuietBible-v1.4.2.apk on your phone and install it (you may need to allow installs
   from your browser or file manager). It installs over any earlier official Quiet Bible and
   keeps your notes. Built in: the King James Version (the main text), the Berean Standard
   Bible, and the English voice "John".

2. Add translations if you want them (Settings > Translation > "Add a translation from a file")
   Translations/
     QuietBible-RV1909.qbbible    Spanish     Reina-Valera 1909
     QuietBible-CUV.qbbible       Chinese     Chinese Union Version (Traditional)
     QuietBible-CUVS.qbbible      Chinese     Chinese Union Version (Simplified)
     QuietBible-ADB.qbbible       Tagalog     Ang Dating Biblia (1905)
     QuietBible-VIE1934.qbbible   Vietnamese  Kinh Thanh 1934 (Ban Truyen Thong)
     QuietBible-LSG.qbbible       French      Louis Segond 1910
     QuietBible-SVD.qbbible       Arabic      Smith & Van Dyck (reads right to left)
     QuietBible-BLIVRE.qbbible    Portuguese  Biblia Livre (Almeida)
     QuietBible-RST.qbbible       Russian     Synodal

3. Add voices if you want them (Settings > Voice > "Add a voice from a file")
   Voices/English
       Kristin (female), Norman (male)
   Voices/Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese, Arabic
       The Supertonic voices come as TWO files, part1 and part2. Add both: you can pick
       them together in the file picker, or one after the other. Together they give each
       language a female and a male voice:
         Spanish Lucia, Mateo     French Claire, Louis     Portuguese Ana, Joao
         Russian Anna, Ivan       Vietnamese Lan, Minh     Arabic Nour, Omar
   Chinese and Tagalog are read by the phone's own voice.
   Voice samples/ has a short recording of each voice.

4. Things to try
   - Tap a verse, then look under "Study": Hebrew/Greek words, cross-references, commentary
     and a Bible dictionary.
   - Tap "Map" at the top: the cross-reference map (Bible Cross-References by Oleksii
     Trekhleb). The back gesture closes it.
   - Library > Research: older texts some readers ask about, each with a description.
